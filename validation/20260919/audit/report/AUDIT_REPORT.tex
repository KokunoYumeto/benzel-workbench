\section{1. Verdict and scope}\label{verdict-and-scope}

\textbf{The frozen turn-10 construction passes this audit.} No
counterexample, failed coefficient identity, invalid source-index
certificate, or omitted canonical parameter was found. The residue-zero
construction is complete at the level of the supplied written,
exact-certificate-assisted argument. The full original Problem 7
assembly uses the explicitly matched generalized-compression theorem for
the residue-two class.

This conclusion is based on a newly written original-cell checker,
separately assembled symbolic expressions, reconstructed source
constraints, exact arithmetic replays, and a fresh review of the
universal mathematical deductions. It is not based on accepting the
producer's recorded \texttt{PASS} values.

The checking implementations are independent of the producer's
verification code. The research and this audit were nevertheless
performed by the same assistant. This is \textbf{not external specialist
acceptance, a Lean kernel certificate, or an independent audit of
Sharma's manuscript}. The audit supplies inspectable evidence for a
subsequent reviewer rather than silently changing a canonical
publication status.

The frozen claim is the existence of original type-103 tilings of \[
W_h(d)=V(d+3h,2d+3h),\qquad d\ge2,\quad 0\le h\le t_d,
\qquad t_d=\frac{d(d-1)}2.
\] The claimed counts are \(t_d-h\) right stones and \(3h(h+d)\) bones.
The supplied code additionally constructs the all-right-stone congruence
class. Its full-P7 statement invokes an external theorem for the
remaining congruence class; this audit retains that dependency.

The audited input is the \texttt{current/} subtree of the cumulative
turn-10 archive, including its turn-7, turn-8, turn-9 and turn-10
mathematical dependencies. All \textbf{131 entries} of its source
manifest matched, with no missing files and no hash mismatches. No
mathematical source was repaired during this audit.

\section{3. Independent check on the original
objects}\label{independent-check-on-the-original-objects}

\subsection{3.1 Cells, tiles and an inverse coordinate
map}\label{cells-tiles-and-an-inverse-coordinate-map}

The checker's original cell is a triple \((i,j,k)\in\mathbb Z^3\) with
\(i+j+k=1\). It reads a candidate pair \((x,y)\) through \[
(x,y)\longmapsto (x,y,1-x-y),
\] whose inverse is projection onto the first two coordinates. These are
Propp's barycentric cell coordinates {[}P{]}.

For a candidate right stone, the new checker obtains the coordinatewise
minimum \(p\) of the three triples and verifies \[
\sum p_i=0,\qquad \text{cells}=\{p+e_1,p+e_2,p+e_3\}.
\] It does not infer the orientation from a supplied label alone. For a
bone, it computes the exact integer center and requires the three cells
to be that center and its two adjacent translates in one of the original
directions. The axial labels are checked against \[
H:(-1,0,1),\quad V:(0,1,-1),\quad D:(1,-1,0),
\] with both signs permitted along each centered bone. Floating-point
coordinates, Boolean coordinates, repeated cells and wrong labels are
rejected.

\subsection{3.2 Region equality without using the producer's
area}\label{region-equality-without-using-the-producers-area}

Write \(L=1-a\), \(U=b-1\). For a fixed first coordinate \(i\), the six
original inequalities give the exact row \[
\begin{split}
j_{\min}&=\max\left(i+L,\left\lceil\frac{1-i-U}{2}\right\rceil,L+1-2i\right),\\
j_{\max}&=\min\left(i+U,\left\lfloor\frac{1-i-L}{2}\right\rfloor,U+1-2i\right).
\end{split}
\] The first coordinate ranges from \[
\left\lceil\frac{1+L-U}{3}\right\rceil
\quad\text{to}\quad
\left\lfloor\frac{1+U-L}{3}\right\rfloor.
\] This follows from \(i=(1-(j-i)+(i-k))/3\). Rows with
\(j_{\min}>j_{\max}\) are empty.

For each row, the checker sorts \emph{every cell occurrence} in the
candidate and compares the sequence with every integer in this interval.
It therefore detects holes and overlapping cells even when they cancel
in a mere area count. It also tests the original inequalities on every
cell directly.

Reflection is applied independently as the barycentric transposition
\((i,j,k)\mapsto(i,k,j)\). It negates and reverses the three
differences, carries \(V(a,b)\) to \(V(b,a)\), preserves right stones
and exchanges the two appropriate bone labels. Its square is the
identity.

\section{4. The unbounded packing
argument}\label{the-unbounded-packing-argument}

The audit rechecked the retained turn-6 packing proof on its original
cell carrier rather than replacing it by the finite sweep.

For the reflected first sector, put \(\Delta=t_d-h\). Its prefix cells
are \[
x=1-2y-r+z,\quad 1\le y\le h,\quad 0\le z\le3y-1,
\qquad r=r(y+\Delta),
\] where \(t_r<y+\Delta\le t_{r+1}\). Since \(y+\Delta\le t_d\), one has
\(1\le r\le d-1\). The three original differences are \[
3y+r-1-z,\qquad r-z,\qquad1-3y-2r+2z.
\] Their actual endpoint bounds establish containment in the reflected
benzel. For a tail row \(y=h+\ell\), the prefix is replaced by \[
x=\ell-2h-d+1+z,\qquad 0\le z<3q_\ell,
\quad q_\ell=\min(h,h+d-\ell).
\] Substitution gives \[
3h+d-1-z,\quad d-3\ell-z,\quad
3\ell-3h-2d+1+2z.
\] The inequalities \(q_\ell\le h\), \(\ell+q_\ell\le h+d\) give every
original bound. Empty rows at \(h=0\) are retained as empty ranges.

Within a sector, different rows are disjoint. A putative overlap with a
rotated sector has \(1\le x<y\). In the prefix/prefix case, the two band
inequalities force equal triangular-block index \(r\) and \(y-x=r\); two
values in one such block differ by at most \(r-1\). The tail/prefix case
forces \(d\le y-x\le d-1\). The tail/tail case forces both
\(2x+y\le3h+d\) and \(2x+y\ge3h+d+3\). The original rotations transfer
these exclusions to every sector pair.

The rank map has a literal inverse. The three differences sum to zero
and are pairwise unequal, because their cyclic differences are congruent
to two modulo three. Rotate the cell so that its unique minimum is the
first difference, \(-r\). The cell is uniquely \[
p_{r,s}=(r-s,-s),\qquad0\le s<r,
\] and its rank is \(t_r+s+1\). Three rotations give exactly three cells
per rank.

The first \(\Delta\) ranks form \(\Omega_\Delta\). To check band
ownership, write \(B=r-s\) and \(c=1-2r+3s\). Of the three
pre-reflection positions, the only possible owners give the
complementary conditions \[
c\le r(B+\Delta)\le r,
\quad\text{or}\quad c\ge1\ \text{and}\ B+\Delta\le t_c.
\] Together these are exactly \(\Delta< t_r+s+1\). The finite tail
misses \(\Omega_\Delta\); its relevant difference is at least \(d\),
whereas a residual cell has differences at most \(d-1\). This checks the
claimed stable complement, not just its cardinality.

The three phase domains give the original area independently as three
copies of a triangle with three disjoint corner triangles removed. Per
phase the count is \[
\binom{d+3h}{2}-2\binom h2-\binom{h+1}{2}.
\] The resulting packing identity is \[
\partial\Gamma_\Delta(d,h)+\mathbf1_{\Omega_\Delta}
=\mathbf1_{W_h(d)}.
\] For triangular \(\Delta=t_m\), the rank inequalities give
\(\Omega_{t_m}=W_0(m)\). The explicit base stones then tile the entire
residual.

\section{5. Exact symbolic equations and their positive
meaning}\label{exact-symbolic-equations-and-their-positive-meaning}

\subsection{5.1 Separate coefficient
engine}\label{separate-coefficient-engine}

The new symbolic program imports no producer symbolic module. It
reconstructs the expression from the frozen literal tables, the original
offsets, and finite geometric sums. Both size parameters remain formal
in \[
\mathbb Z[X^{\pm1},Y^{\pm1},M_x^{\pm1},M_y^{\pm1},K_x^{\pm1},K_y^{\pm1}].
\] Specialization sends \(M_x,M_y,K_x,K_y\) respectively to
\(X^m,Y^m,X^k,Y^k\). Its kernel is generated by these four defining
relations, with quotient inverse induced by the original cell variables.

For a nested family with monomial steps \(u,v\), outer length \(I\), and
inner length \(A+bi\), the exact identity is \[
\sum_{i=0}^{I-1}u^i\sum_{j=0}^{A+bi-1}v^j
=\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{I-1}u^i
-v^A\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{I-1}(uv^b)^i}{1-v}.
\] Its derivation is the finite geometric identity in the inner sum. All
actual lengths are checked to be nonnegative in the original domains;
zero length is not replaced by a negative formal range. Direction-zero
constant sums receive a separate literal treatment.

The affine cell rotation induces the linear map \[
\rho_*P(X,Y)=Y\,P(Y^{-1},XY^{-1}),
\] and reflection induces \(F_*P=Y\,P(XY^{-1},Y^{-1})\). These are the
cell-module maps, not unital ring homomorphisms. The underlying
substitutions and the displayed translation monomial account for their
exact effect on every tile boundary.

All denominator factors use only original cell directions and remain
nonzero under parameter specialization. Clearing them takes place in an
injective localization of an integer Laurent domain. Consequently a zero
numerator gives the claimed original finite cell equation after
specialization. The exact relation \(1/(1-z^{-1})=-z/(1-z)\) is used
when choosing a common denominator; no parameter or tile coefficient is
discarded.

\subsection{5.3 Positivity is an additional checked
step}\label{positivity-is-an-additional-checked-step}

A signed identity alone would not prove a tiling. The audit separately
reconstructs each negative generator's original owner and verifies its
original index bounds, exclusion from the already removed source, and
distinctness from the other negative generators.

There are \textbf{274 original affine tile-owner identities} and
\textbf{6,559 exact nonnegative rational certificates}. The latter are
equations between independently reconstructed integer affine vectors;
each coefficient is checked with \texttt{Fraction}. For a desired bound,

For a forbidden overlap, it yields a negative constant from nonnegative
constraints. A second standard-library checker replays all reconstructed

The parent family lengths have 36 further bounds proved by nonnegative

k=v+2,\qquad m=u+3v+8,
\qquad v=k-2\ge0,\quad u=m-3k-2\ge0.

\section{7. Integral comparison kernels and Split-Zero
support}\label{integral-comparison-kernels-and-split-zero-support}

The audit independently reconstructs the graph attached to two actual
disjoint bone matchings. The columns of \([\partial_-,-\partial_+]\)
give, at each original cell, the difference of its old and new owners. A
singly owned cell is attached to a ground vertex. Thus the signed
incidence matrix is the transpose of an oriented graph incidence matrix
after the explicit old/new sign convention; it is not merely compared by
rank.

An equality between old and new boundaries forces equal coefficients
along each shared-cell edge and zero at each grounded vertex. Its
solutions are exactly constants on closed components, zero on all other
components. This gives the primitive original integer generators of
\(B_-\cap B_+\).

Choose one new-bone pivot in each closed component. The quotient \[
K=B_+/(B_-\cap B_+)
\] has basis the remaining new-bone classes. Its coordinate map
subtracts the pivot coefficient on each closed component; its inverse
puts zero at pivots. Each forest path supplies an integer original-cell
dual. Direct evaluation on every old and new column verifies the
annihilation and Kronecker identities.

The new implementation checked ten actual repair comparisons and two
closed-component controls, producing \textbf{1,026 integer duals}. The
closed controls include a single identical old/new bone and a
three-horizontal-versus-three-vertical tiling of a \(3\times3\) cell
array; they exercise the primitive-relation quotient rather than only
open graphs.

For the full support diagram, a nonbottom empty-release label still
carries the original vacancy support. The scalar actions are \[
e(E,x)=(E,0),\qquad \tau(E,x)=(\bot,0).
\] The incoming boundary and its supported-zero companion have the same
original source and target labels. The fibre quotient descends against
an arbitrary split-linear target because \(f(E,y)+f(E,0)=f(E,y)\); no
cancellation assumption on that target is introduced.

The augmented differential and its actual transitions are \[
\widehat d_E(r,n)=\partial n-rv_E,
\qquad
\widehat j_{EE'}(r,n)=(r,jn+r\eta_{EE'}).
\] The original identity \(v_{E'}=jv_E+\partial\eta_{EE'}\) proves the
cochain square, and addition of the disjoint released-bone sets proves
composition. Charge-one nonnegative integral cycles identify exactly
with original positive patch tilings. Their inclusion into integral
cycles retains every coefficient.

The reconstructed matching quotient maps to the quotient allowing all
contained bones. Its further kernel is
\(\operatorname{im}\partial_{\mathrm{all}}/(B_-+B_+)\). Neither that
object nor the whole comparison kernel is replaced by the distinguished
class. The cohomological deductions agree with the specified positive
cell constructions; they are not used to manufacture a positive lift
from a signed solution alone.

\section{8. The full original problem and the published
dependency}\label{the-full-original-problem-and-the-published-dependency}

Propp's Problem 7 asks for type-103 tileability at nonnegative
Conway--Lagarias invariant {[}P{]}. The audit checked the original
statement and its cell convention rather than relying on the title of a
later manuscript.

In the residue-zero class, after the explicit reflection if needed, \[
d=b-a,\qquad h=(2a-b)/3
\] are integers. The original invariant in stone-cell units is
\(CL=3(t_d-h)\). Admissibility gives \(h\ge0\); nonnegativity gives
\(h\le t_d\). The values \(d=0,1\) would force the invalid parameter
pairs \((0,0)\), \((1,2)\), so every valid pair lands in the audited
\(d\ge2\) construction.

\subsection{8.1 Direct residue-one
construction}\label{direct-residue-one-construction}

For \(a+b\equiv1\pmod3\), take any original cell \(c\), and choose its
unique candidate anchor \(p=c-e_s\) whose difference residue is
\(2-a\pmod3\). The three choices give all three residues; \(p\) has
coordinate sum zero, so its three differences have that same residue.

The cell bounds put those anchor differences in \([-a,b]\). The least
integer of the selected residue in that interval is \(2-a\), and the
greatest is \(b-2\), because \(a+b\equiv1\pmod3\). Hence every cell of
\(\{p+e_1,p+e_2,p+e_3\}\) lies in the original benzel. Every one of
those three cells selects the same anchor. The map from cells to the
unique anchor and offset therefore partitions the region into original
right stones, with the inverse \((p,e_s)\mapsto p+e_s\). This
establishes the full class directly.

\subsection{8.2 Exact residue-two dependency
match}\label{exact-residue-two-dependency-match}

Theorem 1.1 of Defant--Foster--Li--Propp--Young {[}C{]} applies to
\(2\le a\le b\le2a\), \(a+b\equiv2\pmod3\), and gives a count of tilings
using right, rising and falling tiles. Its parameters in this report are
\[
n=b+1-a\ge1,\qquad j=(2a-b-1)/3\ge0,
\] with inverse \((a,b)=(n+3j,2n+3j-1)\). The actual count is \[
\prod_{\ell=1}^{j}
\frac{(2\ell)!(2\ell+2n-2)!}
{(\ell+n-1)!(\ell+n+j-1)!}.
\] Every factorial argument is nonnegative and every factor is positive;
the empty product at \(j=0\) is one. The congruence gives
\(2a-b\equiv1\pmod3\), so \(b\le2a\) really does imply \(j\ge0\). Thus
the theorem provides existence throughout the required domain.

The geometric identification is explicit. With \(\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}\),
send the barycentric cell to \(z=i+j\omega+k\omega^2\). For \(z=x+iy\),
the difference forms are \[
u=-x+y/\sqrt3,\quad v=-2y/\sqrt3,\quad w=x+y/\sqrt3.
\] The unit hexagon offsets \(\pm1,\pm\omega,\pm\omega^2\) change each
form by at most one. Therefore the center bounds \([1-a,b-1]\) are
exactly full-cell containment in the published enclosing hexagon. Its
vertices are the stated \(\omega^r(a\omega+b)\) and
\(-\omega^r(a+b\omega)\). The original bone directions become rising,
vertical and falling directions. The inclusion of the published
right/rising/falling tiles into the four permitted types preserves every
original cell and commutes with incidence.

This is an exact theorem-interface audit. It is \textbf{not a fresh
proof audit of every lemma of the compression paper}. Taking that
published theorem as a cited input, the three congruence classes exhaust
the full original P7 statement.

python audit/run_algebra_checks.py
python audit/run_algebra_checks.py --optimized
python audit/original_cell_check.py --max-d 20
